Wednesday  May 11  1999

Letter
Point missed

I refer to the letter by Sueli Roche on her experience of breastfeeding in the Peninsula Hotel ("Breastfeeding incident mars happy day", South China Morning Post, May 4).

When I go to a hotel for a meal I do not wish to see a woman breastfeeding at the next table. That breastfeeding is natural is beside the point. Going to the toilet or having sex are equally natural but I would not wish to see these activities either.

DAVID OLLEREARNSHAW
Yuen Long
