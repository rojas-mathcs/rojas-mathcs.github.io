    Software

    • count.map: Maple code implementing the first (Las Vegas) randomized polynomial-time algorithm to the count the number of roots of a univariate polynomial over a prime power ring.
    • CKTPOSFEAS: Matlab code for deciding feasibility for n-variate (n+2)-nomials in polynomial-time (relative to the spares encoding).
    • HKPLOT: Visualizing A-Discriminants for generic (n+3)-sets in Zn.
    • AMOEBA: Visualizing the zero sets of bivariate polynomials over non-Archimedean complete fields (co-authored with Zhigang Zhang).

    Impact of COVID-19 on Productivity

    Rojas was working at the National Science Foundation (starting Sep. 3, 2019) when the COVID-19 pandemic struck. After some negotiation, he was allowed to return to College Station to continue his work remotely until the end of his term (Sept. 2, 2020).

    As a result of the pandemic, Rojas' classes were a mixture of Zoom classes and in-person classes. Around October 2019, he was exposed to COVID-19 (ultimately testing negative) and followed proper distancing/quaranting protocol to continue his teaching without interruption.

    Rojas did his best to adapt to Zoom teaching: Fall 2020 was his first time teaching remotely. Zoom-fatigue is well-documented as an obstruction to good teaching and Rojas observed that his strengths with improvisation and motivating students were useful to combat these handicaps.

    Two of Rojas' research international invitations were completely cancelled due to the pandemic (FOCM conference in Vancouver and Mathematics and Music Symposium in Braunschweig, Germany).
