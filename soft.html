A List of Some Software by Maurice and his Students

If you have trouble using a particular package below, then please e-mail me at rojas@math.tamu.edu .

  • countk: a short Maple procedure that counts the number of roots in Z/(pk) of a univariate polynomial with integer coefficients, in time polynomial d+k log(p). Written by Leann Kopp and Natalie Randall, during a Summer 2018 REU at Texas A&M, mentored by J. Maurice Rojas
  • CRYSTAL: Visualizing Mixed Subdivisions of 1, 2, 3, or 4 polytopes in three dimensions (co-authored with Zhigang Zhang).
  • AMOEBA: Visualizing the zero sets of bivariate polynomials over non-Archimedean complete fields (co-authored with Zhigang Zhang).
  • 3 X 2 Sparse Resultants (Beta Version in Matlab, co-authored with Christopher Wong)
  • 3 X 2 (Determininstic) Mixed Subdivisions via the MaxPinch Heuristic (Matlab)
  • 2 X 2 (Determininstic) Mixed Subdivisions via the MaxPinch Heuristic (Matlab)
  • Mixed Area computation in Matlab
Much of the software above has been funded in part by the National Science Foundation, through grants DMS-0138446, DMS-0211458, CCF-1409020, and REU site grants DMS-1460766 and DMS-1757872 (to name just a few of my grants).
